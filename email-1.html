Product Overview

ATax Software Solutions is a income tax preparation software company serving thousands of tax offices across the United States. The project involved designing and developing a responsive HTML email template to help their existing and potential clients understand and choose between the different platform options AccuTax offers.

Brief

The goal was to create a professional, branded email that introduces AccuTax's platform lineup Desktop, The Jumper, AccuCloud, and Business - in a clear and digestible format. The email needed to feel consistent with the existing website, use the company's brand colors, and work across all major email clients and devices.

Design & Development

The template was built in pure HTML and CSS, following email-safe standards compatible with Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and mobile clients. The layout uses a single-column structure with modular platform cards, keeping the content easy to scan and the hierarchy clear. Brand colors - navy, orange, and white — are applied consistently throughout to maintain a strong visual connection to the company website.

Outcome
  • Fully responsive HTML/CSS email template
  • Individual platform cards with images, feature tags, and CTAs
  • Brand-aligned color system and typography
  • Single-column layout for maximum compatibility across email clients
