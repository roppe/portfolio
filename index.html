About Me
Designer
& Builder

I'm a UI/UX graphic and web designer based in Serbia, with almost 10 years of experience designing digital products, platforms,websites and visual assets. I've worked with marketing agencies, and product teams, both in agency environments and as a long-term freelance partner.I also have experience in project coordination, supporting development workflows, organizing tasks for teams, and mentoring junior designers.

My work covers UX/UI,product,web, and visual design, with hands-on experience taking projects from early concepts and wireframes through to final UI, front-end implementation, and real-world iteration. Over the last four years, I’ve focused heavily on complex platforms and applications, including cybersecurity products.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked closely with cross-functional teams including developers, product managers, copywriters, marketers, SEO specialists, and PPC teams. This has given me a strong understanding of how design connects with communication, acquisition, and overall business performance.

With a background in front-end development, I design solutions that are practical to build and easy to maintain. I approach every project as a problem to be solved, I combine design thinking with technical and marketing awareness to deliver solutions that are not only visually strong, but also functional, clear, and aligned with real business outcomes.

What I Do

My Services

Blending UX/UI, web, product, & visual design with marketing awareness to create effective digital experiences.

01
UI/UX Design

Clear, usable, and scalable interfaces for digital products, platforms, and applications. Focused on solving real user and business problems through thoughtful UX and strong visual hierarchy.

  • End-to-end UX/UI for platforms & applications
  • User research, flows & wireframes
  • High-fidelity UI in Figma
  • Design systems & reusable components
  • Design alignment with development and product requirements
02
Web Design

Modern, user-focused websites combining strong visual design with structured content, clear navigation, and business goals. With front-end know-how, I design what's actually buildable.

  • Website & landing page,design
  • Wireframes to final responsive web UI
  • Redesigning existing websites
  • Desktop, tablet, and mobile responsive
  • Marketing pages,site content, & emails
03
Front-end Dev

Clean, responsive, and maintainable front-end solutions that bring designs to life accurately and efficiently. Bridging the gap between visual intent and technical execution.

  • HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, & JavaScript for interactive functionality
  • WordPress design/dev & maintenance
  • Figma / PSD to working interfaces
  • UI fixes, SEO & performance
  • AI-assisted development tools
04
Graphic Design

Clear, consistent, and recognizable visual systems supporting brands across digital and print. Strong layout, typography, and visual hierarchy ensuring designs communicate effectively.

  • Brand identity & visual systems
  • Digital and print materials
  • Marketing assets & presentations
  • Logos, icons & brand elements
  • Tools: Figma, Adobe Creative Suite
How I Work

My Process

A clear process turns ideas into usable, effective solutions — step by step, from problem to product.

01
Discover & Define

I start by understanding the project goals, target users, and business context. I review requirements, research existing solutions, and help define a clear direction for the project before execution begins.

02
Structure & Create

I shape the solution through UX structure, content flow, wireframes, and visual design. This phase focuses on clarity, usability, and aligning design with business and communication goals.

03
Deliver & Improve

I prepare final design outputs and collaborate with developers, marketing, and product teams to ensure accurate implementation. After launch, I support improvements based on feedback and real-world performance./p>
