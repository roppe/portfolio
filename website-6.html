About Project

Project for NYC Outsourcing Agency and their client. A full redesign and development of 60+ HTML web pages. The previous website was outdated, mostly plain text, and not optimized for different screen sizes. The goal was a complete visual and structural redesign with improved UX/UI, using a combination of vector illustrations and stock photography to better explain processes and present products across the website. Each page was redesigned to be more user-friendly, responsive, and conversion-focused.
