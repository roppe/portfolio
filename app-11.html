Product Overview

BSO Academy is a educational platform dedicated to empowering individuals with the knowledge they need to navigate the digital world safely. As part of the mission to make online safety education accessible to everyone, the platform offers a wide range of educational content in a clear, effective, and easy-to-understand format.

BSO provides users with the tools and insights necessary to recognize, avoid, and respond to online scams, helping people better protect their digital security.

My Role

Led the end-to-end UX/UI design process from research and concept to final interface. Defined the content structure, user flows, and layouts with a strong focus on clarity, accessibility, and ease of use for a non-technical audience.

UX Challenges
  • Designing for older and less tech-savvy users
  • Simplifying complex and sensitive topics into clear, supportive flows
  • Creating a distraction-free learning experience
  • Structuring large amounts of educational content in an intuitive way
Outcome
  • Delivered a clean, accessible, and user-friendly learning platform
