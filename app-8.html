App & Product design

BECYBER
Platform

Industry Cybersecurity / Threat Intelligence & Security Awareness Platform/
Product Overview

BeCyber is a cybersecurity platform that combines threat intelligence and security awareness in a single system. It enables organizations to detect external exposure through dark web and open-source monitoring while training employees with phishing simulations, awareness courses, and progress tracking in an integrated LMS.

My Role

Led the UX/UI design across the platform in collaboration with product management, the CTO, and the development team - from early concepts to production and continuous improvements.

  • Translated complex cybersecurity workflows into clear, scalable interfaces
  • Designed data-driven dashboards, LMS content creation flows, and student-facing training screens
  • Created modular layouts to support platform growth and feature expansion
UX Challenges
  • Unifying three different product areas into one consistent experience: Intelligence, LMS configuration, and end-user training required a shared design system and clear navigation logic.
  • Designing for both analytical and learning workflows: The platform had to support deep data analysis while also providing simple, engaging training experiences.
  • Scalability for organizations and users: Layouts and components were built to handle growing data, new content types, and multi-organization structures.
  • Balancing powerful functionality with usability: Advanced cybersecurity features needed to remain understandable for non-technical users.
Outcome
  • Clear and structured threat intelligence workflows
  • Scalable LMS for awareness training and phishing simulations
  • Engaging and easy-to-follow student learning experience
  • Consistent UI across multiple complex product areas
