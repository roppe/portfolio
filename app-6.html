Product Overview

HackersRange is the ultimate cybersecurity training and up-skilling platform, combining real-world simulations with gamification to make practical, hands-on expertise accessible while keeping the experience educational, engaging, competitive, and fun.

The platform supports onboarding, skill evaluation, and real-world scenario practice through a multi-role system with dedicated admin and student experiences.

The Admin Environment

The Admin side functions as an all-in-one LMS and content management system where organizations and instructors create cyber labs, build gamified training, manage users and classes, assign learning paths, and track performance through advanced reporting. It is a data-heavy, multi-role environment designed for efficient configuration, scalability, and full control over the learning experience.

My Role

I was responsible for the UX/UI design of the admin platform, working closely with the Product Manager, CTO, and development team from the early product definition phase through launch and continuous iteration. Based on product requirements and feature discussions, I translated complex functionality into clear, scalable interfaces. This collaborative process ensured that business goals, technical constraints, and user needs were aligned in the final product.

UX Challenges
  • Designing for a growing first-version product — the platform was continuously evolving, requiring frequent updates, UI refinements, and structural improvements while maintaining consistency across all modules.
  • Planning scalable layouts — the interface had to support new features and expanding functionality, so screens were designed with flexible structures and efficient space usage.
  • Managing complex and data-heavy views — admin pages such as reports and content builders required clear hierarchy and smart grouping to reduce cognitive load.
  • Balancing powerful functionality with usability — advanced configuration options needed to remain easy to use for users with different levels of technical knowledge.
Outcome
  • Enabled efficient creation and management of cyber training content.
  • Established a consistent UI across multiple system modules.
  • Improved clarity of complex workflows for both non-technical and technical users.
  • Supported real-time monitoring and structured learning management at scale.
